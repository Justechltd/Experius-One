Education & Training

Connect the journey from first interest to enrolment and beyond.

People ask about courses, dates and fees, and many quietly disappear. Those who enrol get registered, added to groups and followed through classes, often by hand. We help learning businesses connect those steps.

Experius is a business systems and content studio. Our team is based in Bangladesh and works with service businesses locally and internationally.

Does this feel familiar?

You may recognise some of these

  • Interest goes quiet

    People ask about fees and dates, then nobody follows up.

  • Registration is scattered

    Paper forms, online forms and chat messages, all handled differently.

  • Details are typed in twice

    Student information is copied into several sheets and tools.

  • Communication lives in many groups

    Class updates are hard to find, for students and for staff.

  • Progress is hard to see

    Attendance and progress sit in different places, or nowhere.

  • Renewals depend on memory

    Nobody reminds students when the next course starts.

The journey

How a learner typically moves through, and where it can break

An illustrative journey. Yours will differ, which is why we map it first.

StepWhere it often breaks
  1. ContentSomeone discovers your teaching.
  2. InterestThey want to know more.
  3. Inquiry

    Can break here: leads ask questions and are never followed up.

  4. Trial or consultationA taster class or a conversation.
  5. Registration

    Can break here: details are collected in several ways and entered twice.

  6. EnrolmentPayment and a confirmed place.
  7. Classes

    Can break here: student communication is scattered across groups and chats.

  8. Progress

    Can break here: attendance and progress are hard to track.

  9. RenewalThey take the next course, or recommend you.
What a better flow could look like

From interest to enrolment, without the re-typing

We usually connect what you have, rather than introduce a large learning platform. If you need one, we'll help you choose.

  • Every course inquiry in one list, with a follow-up date
  • One registration form that feeds your student list directly
  • Automatic confirmations, joining details and reminders
  • One clear channel for class communication
  • A simple view of attendance and progress
  • A reminder to invite students to the next course

Questions people ask

Do you build learning management systems?

Not by default. Most learning businesses need inquiries, registration and communication connected first. If you need a full learning platform, we'll help you choose and connect an existing one.

What about student information?

We agree what's collected, why, who can see it and where it lives before building anything, with extra care for information about children.

We run classes online and in person. Does that matter?

It changes the details, not the approach. We map how both actually work in your business.

Where do learners drop off in your business?

Tell us how people find you, register and stay. We'll help you see what's worth connecting first.
