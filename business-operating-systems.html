Business Operating Systems

Connect the moving parts of your business.

Inquiries, follow-ups, bookings, onboarding and reporting shouldn't depend on memory and copy-paste. We map how work actually moves through your business, then build the smallest connected system that makes it clearer.

Experius is a business systems and content studio. Our team is based in Bangladesh and works with service businesses locally and internationally.

The journey a good operating system supports

What this can cover

Start from the problem, not the software

For each area: the problem you may recognise, what a better process looks like, what might be built, and what we need to understand before recommending anything.

Lead capture and management

The problem
Inquiries arrive through WhatsApp, Messenger, calls and forms, and nobody sees them all.
Better process
Every inquiry lands in one list, whatever channel it came from.
What might be built
Form and channel capture into a shared inquiry record.
We need to understand
Where inquiries really arrive, and how many a week.

CRM and pipeline

The problem
Follow-up depends on memory, and prospects quietly go cold.
Better process
Each prospect has a stage, an owner and a next action with a date.
What might be built
A simple pipeline with reminders, sized to your team.
We need to understand
Your sales steps, who sells, and what "qualified" means for you.

Booking and registration

The problem
Scheduling, confirming and reminding take endless back-and-forth.
Better process
People book or register in a few clear steps, and reminders go out on their own.
What might be built
A booking or registration flow linked to the customer record.
We need to understand
Capacity, practitioners or classes, and your cancellation rules.

Client onboarding

The problem
Every new client gets a slightly different start, depending on who's busy.
Better process
A consistent welcome: the same information, forms and first steps every time.
What might be built
An onboarding checklist with templates and automatic hand-offs.
We need to understand
What a good first week looks like for your clients.

Dashboards and reporting

The problem
You can't see what's happening without asking someone.
Better process
A few numbers that matter, visible to the people who act on them.
What might be built
A simple dashboard drawn from the records you already keep.
We need to understand
Which decisions you make each week, and with what information.

Workflow automation

The problem
The team retypes the same information and sends the same messages by hand.
Better process
Repetitive steps happen on their own, and a person stays responsible for the outcome.
What might be built
Automations between the tools you use, with clear failure alerts.
We need to understand
Which steps are truly repetitive, and which need human judgement.

Website-to-operations connections

The problem
Your website collects information that someone then copies into a spreadsheet.
Better process
What a visitor submits reaches the right person and record directly.
What might be built
Form, booking and inquiry connections from the site to your system.
We need to understand
What the website's job is, and what happens after someone submits.

Questions people ask

Will you replace the tools we use now?

Only if they're the problem. We choose tools last, after understanding the process. Often the right system is the tools you have, connected properly, plus one or two small additions.

Which tools do you build with?

It depends on your process, team and budget. That might be a well-structured spreadsheet, a CRM, an automation platform, or a small custom application when nothing off the shelf fits. We'll explain the trade-offs in plain language.

Who owns the system when you're done?

You do. We hand over access, documentation and training, and agree support arrangements before we start, so the system isn't dependent on us.

Do you use automation and AI?

Where it removes repetitive work and a person stays responsible for the result. We don't add AI for its own sake, and we'll tell you when a simpler approach is better.

What about sensitive customer or patient information?

Before designing any form or database, we agree what's collected, why, who can see it and where it lives. Health records and other regulated information need an explicit privacy, security and legal review before anything is built.

Where does work get stuck in your business?

Tell us how inquiries, follow-ups and bookings work today. We'll help you see what's worth connecting first.
