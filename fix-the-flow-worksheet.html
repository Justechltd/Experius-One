Fix the Flow: find where things get lost.

Fifteen minutes, one pen. Follow your last five inquiries through the business and answer four questions for each. Print this page, or save it as a PDF.

The four questions

  1. Where does it arrive?WhatsApp, a call, the website form, Instagram, email…
  2. Where is it recorded?A list, a spreadsheet, a notebook, someone's memory, nowhere.
  3. Who owns it?One named person, or "whoever sees it".
  4. What happens next, and when?A clear next step with a date, or "it depends".
