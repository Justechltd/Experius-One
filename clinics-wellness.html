Clinics & Wellness

Make the journey from first inquiry to follow-up feel more connected.

Patients get in touch in many ways, and your team juggles calls, messages and schedules. We help practices see where requests and follow-ups slip, and connect the steps around them, so your team spends less time coordinating.

Experius is a business systems and content studio. Our team is based in Bangladesh and works with service businesses locally and internationally.

Does this feel familiar?

You may recognise some of these

  • Requests arrive in many places

    Phone, WhatsApp, Facebook and the website, each checked by someone different.

  • The same details get typed again

    Front-desk staff re-enter information from messages and forms.

  • Nobody owns follow-up

    After a first visit, it's unclear who checks in or rebooks.

  • Reminders go out by hand

    When the front desk is busy, they don't go out at all.

  • The website doesn't explain how to book

    So people call or message to ask, adding to the queue.

  • Schedules live in different places

    Each practitioner keeps their own, and the team pieces it together.

The journey

How a patient typically moves through a practice, and where it can break

An illustrative journey. Every practice is different, which is why we map yours first.

StepWhere it often breaks
  1. Discovery

    Can break here: the website's information is disconnected from how booking actually works.

  2. Inquiry

    Can break here: inquiries aren't tracked in one place, so some get no reply.

  3. Assessment or appointment request

    Can break here: requests need too many manual steps to confirm.

  4. BookingA time is confirmed with the right practitioner.
  5. VisitThe patient is seen.
  6. Follow-up

    Can break here: no clear owner for check-ins, rebooking or reminders.

  7. Progress and retentionOngoing care, where it's needed.
  8. ReferralPatients recommend the practice to others.
What a better flow could look like

One list, clear owners, reminders that go out on their own

We work alongside the clinical software you already use. We don't replace it, and we don't promise clinical outcomes.

  • Every inquiry and appointment request in one list, whatever the channel
  • An owner and a next action on every request
  • A clear booking path on the website, linked to how the front desk works
  • Automatic confirmations and reminders
  • A follow-up step after visits, with someone responsible
  • A simple view of requests, bookings and follow-ups for the practice owner

How we handle patient information

  • Before designing any form or database, we agree what is collected, why, who can see it and where it lives.
  • Health records and other regulated workflows need an explicit privacy, security and legal review before anything is built.
  • Our demonstrations use example data only. We never show real patient information.

Questions people ask

Will you replace our clinic management software?

No. We work around and alongside it, on the parts before and after the appointment, where requests and follow-ups tend to get lost.

Can patients book online?

Often, yes, if it suits how your practitioners work. Some practices prefer online requests that the front desk confirms. We'll recommend what fits your capacity and rules.

Do you work with small practices?

Yes. A single-practitioner clinic and a multi-practitioner centre have different needs, and we size the system to yours.

Where do requests slip in your practice?

Tell us how patients get in touch and get booked today. We'll help you see what's worth connecting first.
