    What the Business Review is

    A focused conversation about how your business currently attracts, converts and serves customers. We'll understand what is working, where friction may exist and what would be worth improving first. If Experius is a good fit, we'll outline a sensible next step. If it isn't, we'll say so.

    • 25–30 minutes, online, or another format we agree
    • With someone who'll work on your business
    • Free. If there's a sensible next step, it's scoped and priced in writing before anything starts

    It is

    • A first conversation
    • A chance to understand your context
    • A way to find the most relevant problem
    • An honest talk about fit and next steps

    It isn't

    • A full audit delivered for free
    • A commitment to buy
    • A promise of guaranteed growth
    • A generic sales presentation

    What happens after you send this

    1. We read your request

      Someone on the team looks at your business and what you've told us before replying.

    2. We reply within 2 working days

      By email. If a conversation makes sense, we suggest times. If we're not the right fit, we tell you, and point you somewhere useful if we can.

    3. We talk

      25–30 minutes about your business and what's worth improving first.

    Before you send it

    Does the Business Review cost anything?

    No. The Business Review is free. If we both see a fit, we'll suggest a next step and agree its scope and price in writing before any work starts. There's no obligation to go ahead.

    Who will I talk to?

    Someone from the team who would actually work on your business, usually Samir, our Founder & CEO, for a first conversation.

    Do I need to prepare anything?

    No. It helps to think of one recent example of something that slipped: an inquiry nobody replied to, a booking that took too long. Please don't send confidential documents or customer data.
