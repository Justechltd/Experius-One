The journey

How a client typically reaches you, and where it can break

An illustrative journey. Yours will differ, which is why we map it first.

StepWhere it often breaks
  1. ExpertiseWhat you know and how you help.
  2. Content

    Can break here: content shows knowledge but doesn't explain the offer or the next step.

  3. Audience and trustPeople follow, read and start to trust you.
  4. Inquiry

    Can break here: inquiries are scattered across channels and some never get a reply.

  5. Consultation

    Can break here: scheduling and preparation are arranged by hand, message by message.

  6. ProposalWhat you'll do together, and the investment.
  7. ClientThey say yes.
  8. Onboarding

    Can break here: each client gets a different start, and their information is hard to keep organised.

What a better flow could look like

Clear content, one inquiry path, a consistent start

Not every business needs all of this. We find the one or two changes that matter most and start there.

  • Content that explains one problem and one offer clearly, with a next step
  • One inquiry list, whichever channel someone used
  • Consultations booked in a few clicks, with a short intake beforehand
  • A proposal template and a clear yes-or-no step
  • The same onboarding sequence for every new client
  • One place for client notes and resources

Questions people ask

I work on my own. Is this too much for me?

Often solo practitioners benefit most, because everything depends on them. We size the system to you: sometimes that's a clear booking page and a simple inquiry list, nothing more.

I'm a therapist. What about client confidentiality?

We agree what information is collected, why, who can see it and where it lives before designing anything. Clinical notes and records need their own privacy and legal review, and we'll tell you where they shouldn't go.

Will this make me more dependent on social media?

The opposite, ideally. Social channels bring attention; your website, inquiry list and email are what you own. We help you move interest from rented channels to your own.

Is your expertise turning into enough conversations?

Tell us how clients find you and what happens next. We'll help you see where the journey could be clearer.
