What a better flow could look like

A quick, kind first reply, one record per child, and parents who know what's next

We organize the work around the sessions. We don't assess children, give clinical advice or promise outcomes; that stays with your specialists.

  • Every parent inquiry in one list, whatever the channel, with an owner and a next step
  • Clear answers to common questions on the website and in quick replies
  • A simple intake form, so families share their child's background once
  • Assessment and session booking with confirmations and reminders
  • Schedule changes shared with parents and therapists straight away
  • A regular rhythm for progress updates, so no family is forgotten

How we handle children's information

  • Before designing any form or database, we agree with you what is collected, why, who can see it and where it lives. Parents' consent comes first.
  • Assessment reports, diagnoses and other sensitive records need an explicit privacy and security review before anything is built.
  • We never show a child's face or name in our work without the family's written consent.
  • We don't make claims about diagnoses or outcomes, on your website or in your content.

Questions people ask

Do you give advice about children or therapy? {{icon:plus}}

No. Your specialists decide everything about assessment and support. We work on the parts around it: how families reach you, how sessions are booked and how information is kept and shared.

Our parents mostly use WhatsApp and Facebook. Does that work? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. We start from the channels your families already use and bring the messages into one place for your team, rather than asking parents to change.

We are a small centre or a non-profit. Is this for us? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. A small centre needs a simpler system than a large school, and we size it to your team and budget. The Business Review is free and helps you see what's worth doing first.

Can you help us explain our work to parents? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. Clear, gentle content that helps parents understand what you do and what the first step is often reduces the questions your team has to answer. How we approach content.
