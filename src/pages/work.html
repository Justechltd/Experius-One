Experiments

What we're testing on our own business

Things we try on Experius first, written up honestly, including what didn't work.

ExperimentLead finding

Where do businesses without a website actually live?

What we tried
We searched Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn for service businesses that could use a website, and checked each one by hand.
What happened
On Instagram, 61 of the 63 businesses we checked had no website. On Facebook it was 149 of 351. On LinkedIn, almost every company already had one.
What we learned
The right channel depends on what you sell. We stopped using LinkedIn to find website prospects and put that time into Instagram.
ExperimentOur own tool

ContentOps: running content for several brands in one place

What we tried
We built our own content tool: one calendar per brand, a status for every post, and a review link clients open without logging in.
What happened
It's live, and we've loaded real monthly calendars into it. The client review loop hasn't been proven end to end with a real client yet.
What we learned
The hard part isn't the calendar. It's getting a clear yes or no from the client on time, which is what we're testing next.
