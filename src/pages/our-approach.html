Our approach

Understand first. Build with purpose. Improve through evidence.

Five steps, every time. You always know where we are and why.

  1. 01 Understand
  2. 02 Demonstrate
  3. 03 Solve
  4. 04 Document
  5. 05 Repeat
  1. 01

    Understand

    We learn how your business works. No recommendations yet.

    • Talks with your team
    • A look at your tools and messages
    The questions we ask {{icon:plus}}
    • What is the business trying to achieve?
    • Who does it serve?
    • How do customers move through it today?
    • Where does information get lost?
    • What is manual, repetitive or unclear?
    • What constraints matter: budget, people, time?
  2. 02

    Demonstrate

    We show you where it breaks, before anything is built.

    • A journey map
    • The friction points
    • A small prototype
    Everything you might get {{icon:plus}}
    • A customer journey map
    • Your current workflow, drawn out
    • The friction points we found
    • A small prototype of the improvement
    • An opportunity summary with priorities
  3. 03

    Solve

    We build the smallest system that fixes it.

    • Scope and price in writing
    • Testing with your team
    • Training and handover
    How we build {{icon:plus}}
    • Fit the solution to the process
    • Keep it understandable
    • Avoid unnecessary complexity
    • Choose technology last
    • Make ownership and handover clear
  4. 04

    Document

    We write it down, so you own it.

    • The problem and our decisions
    • How to use the system
    • What changed
    What we document {{icon:plus}}
    • The starting situation and the problem
    • The decisions we made, and why
    • The system, and how people use it
    • What changed, measured where possible
    • The lessons

    If we'd like to share your project publicly, we ask first, and you approve what's shown.

  5. 05

    Repeat

    We review it in real use. More only if results earn it.

    • A review after launch
    • Fixes where it doesn't fit
    • Your call on what's next
