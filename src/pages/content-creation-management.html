Questions people ask

Do I have to be on camera? {{icon:plus}}

Not necessarily. Your expertise has to come through, but there are many formats: written, voice, visual explainers, or video with someone else presenting. We'll find what fits you and your audience.

Will it sound like me? {{icon:plus}}

That's the point. We start from how you explain things to clients, and you approve everything before it's published.

Who owns the content? {{icon:plus}}

You do. Ownership of raw files and finished content is set out in the agreement before we start.

Which languages do you produce in? {{icon:plus}}

English, and natural Bangla-English for audiences in Bangladesh.
