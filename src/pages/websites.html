Six jobs a website can do

Most sites need two or three of these done well

  • 01

    Clarify the offer

    A visitor understands what you do, and for whom, in seconds.

  • 02

    Build trust

    Real work, real people and honest explanations, instead of claims.

  • 03

    Answer important questions

    The questions that stop people contacting you get answered before they ask.

  • 04

    Qualify inquiries

    The right people get in touch with the information you need to help them.

  • 05

    Enable bookings or registrations

    People can take the next step without a round of messages.

  • 06

    Connect to your team

    What visitors submit reaches the right person and record, without copy-paste.

How we approach a website

Job first, pages second

  1. Define the job

    Agree what the website must achieve for the business.

  2. Map the journey

    How visitors arrive, what they need to know, where they go next.

  3. Write and design

    Content first, then a design that makes it clear.

  4. Connect

    Forms, bookings and inquiries reach your team's system.

  5. Measure

    Track qualified inquiries, not just visits.

Questions people ask

Can you improve our existing website instead of rebuilding it? {{icon:plus}}

Often, yes. If the site's job is clear and the foundations are sound, focused changes to content and connections can matter more than a redesign.

Which platform will you use? {{icon:plus}}

The one that suits your team's ability to maintain it. We'll explain the options and their trade-offs before recommending one.

Can we update the website ourselves? {{icon:plus}}

That's usually the goal. We hand over training and documentation for the parts your team will change regularly.
