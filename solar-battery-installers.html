Solar & Battery Installers

Keep every lead, survey and installation moving.

From first inquiry to years of after-sales. Solar and battery buyers take time to decide. They need clear explanations, a site visit, a quote and patient follow-up, and after installation they need service for years. We help installers make that decision easier with content, and keep the whole journey organized with connected systems.

Does this feel familiar?

You may recognize some of these

  • Buyers ask the same questions

    System size, batteries, what happens on install day, again and again.

  • Inquiries come from everywhere

    Ads, referrals, calls, forms and messages, with no single owner.

  • Quotes go cold

    They go out, and follow-up depends on someone remembering.

  • Survey details stay on phones

    Photos and measurements live with whoever did the visit.

  • Installations run on chat threads

    Dates, crews and stock are coordinated message by message.

  • After handover, you lose touch

    Service visits, warranty questions and referral asks get lost.

The journey

A long journey, with many places to lose a customer

An illustrative journey based on common installer workflows. Yours will differ, which is why we map it first.

StepWhere it often breaks
  1. Content or referral

    Can break here: buyers can't find clear answers, so they hesitate or ask the same questions every time.

  2. Inquiry

    Can break here: inquiries arrive through several channels and nobody owns them.

  3. QualificationLocation, energy use, roof and goals.
  4. Site survey

    Can break here: photos and measurements stay on technicians' phones.

  5. System design and quote

    Can break here: quotes are rebuilt by hand in spreadsheets.

  6. Follow-up and financing

    Can break here: follow-up depends on memory, over a long decision.

  7. ContractThe customer says yes.
  8. Installation scheduling

    Can break here: dates, crews and stock are coordinated in chat threads.

  9. Commissioning and handoverThe system is switched on and explained.
  10. Monitoring and service

    Can break here: no service record, so maintenance and warranty questions get lost.

  11. ReferralHappy customers recommend you, if someone asks.

Questions people ask

Do you know our local rebates and regulations?

No, and we won't pretend to. Schemes and rules differ by country and state and change often. Your team owns technical and regulatory accuracy; we make it clear, consistent and easy to understand, and you approve every claim.

Which markets do you work in?

We work with installers internationally, online, from our team in Bangladesh.

How do you handle time zones?

We agree regular meeting times and response expectations at the start, and plan our working hours around them.

Do you replace our CRM or design software?

Not unless it's the problem. Many installers already have good tools that aren't connected. We start by mapping how a customer actually moves through your business.

Where do your customers go quiet?

Tell us how leads, surveys, quotes and installations work today. We'll help you see where content or a better system would make the biggest difference.
